Giving back

Sheilah runs her philanthropic work through the Sheilah Gashumba Foundation. It began in 2012 as Young Bodies Big Hearts, a charity of young people who believe in giving back to society and helping the less privileged, which she later rebranded as the foundation.

Sheilah Gashumba Foundation

Her foundation carries her charity initiatives forward, bringing her platform and partners together to support communities in Uganda.

Young Bodies Big Hearts

Where it started: a group of young people that held yearly giving events and supported children’s homes, including Sanyu Babies Home and God’s Image Ministry.
