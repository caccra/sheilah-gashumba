DJ Lil Stunner

“This isn’t just a side hustle or trend. I’ve trained, I’ve studied, and I’m ready to take over.”

  • Trained for it

    Her DJ journey began during the COVID-19 lockdown, with one-on-one lessons from DJ Edwizzy Selekta. In 2024 she trained at RISE DJ Academy in Johannesburg, one of Africa’s premier DJ schools.

  • The sound

    Amapiano, Afro house and 3 Step, with Afro-soul and Ugandan rhythms. Built for club nights, festival stages and brand launches.
