Meet Sheilah

Sheilah Carol Gashumba, known as the Lil Stunner, is a Ugandan media personality, TV presenter, radio host, events host, DJ, brand ambassador and philanthropist.

Her story

Sheilah’s career began at the age of 10, presenting Kids News on WBS TV in 2007. By 11 she was hosting Teens Club, winning hearts across the country and making headlines as the youngest journalist accredited to cover the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) in Kampala.

From there she built a career that spans television presenting, entertainment broadcasting, event hosting, social media influence and commercial brand partnerships. After years with Uganda’s leading stations, she stepped away from media houses to focus on her own brand, platforms and businesses.

Along the way she has built one of the most loyal followings in Ugandan entertainment. She was the first Ugandan TV presenter to reach a million followers on Facebook, and is often written about as Sheila Gashumba.

A hardworking businesswoman and a fashion trendsetter, she once ran a luxury hair business and today owns the luxury fashion store Gash Luxe. She also gives back through the Sheilah Gashumba Foundation.

DJ Lil Stunner

Her passion for music gave birth to DJ Lil Stunner.

Her DJ journey began during the COVID-19 lockdown, with one-on-one lessons from DJ Edwizzy Selekta. Determined to hone her craft, she later enrolled at RISE DJ Academy in Johannesburg, one of Africa’s premier DJ schools, and in 2024, while balancing a packed professional schedule, immersed herself in both the technical and creative sides of DJing.

Behind the decks she plays amapiano, Afro house and 3 Step. She headlined the 2025 Vumbula Uganda Festival, and in August 2026 released her first YouTube DJ mix with Don Julio 1942.

Brand partnerships and commercial work

In September 2025, Don Julio 1942 announced Sheilah Gashumba, “Lil Stunner”, as its official ambassador in Uganda.

Since then she has fronted the brand’s nightlife events, photoshoots and appearances, and released music mixes in partnership with it, including her first YouTube DJ mix in August 2026.

  • Don Julio 1942Official ambassador in Uganda, from September 2025
  • Africell UgandaAmbassador for the Triple Data promotion, 2017

Brands work with her for her reach, her style and her ability to fill a room, across campaigns, launches, ambassadorships and appearances. She builds her own brands too, from her earlier luxury hair business to her luxury fashion store, Gash Luxe.

Sheilah the philanthropist

In 2012 Sheilah started Young Bodies Big Hearts, a charity of young people who believe in giving back to society and helping the less privileged.

The group held yearly giving events and supported children’s homes, including Sanyu Babies Home and God’s Image Ministry. As time went on, she rebranded it as the Sheilah Gashumba Foundation, which carries her charity initiatives forward, bringing her platform and partners together to support communities in Uganda.
